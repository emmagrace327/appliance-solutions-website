Common Refrigerator & Freezer Repair Problems We Fix

Refrigerator Not Cooling

The most urgent call we get. A fridge that runs but doesn't cool usually points to a failing compressor relay, a clogged condenser coil, or a malfunctioning thermostat. We test the sealed system on-site and determine whether a simple relay swap or a deeper compressor repair is needed. In most cases, we resolve this in a single visit.

Ice Buildup in Freezer

A wall of frost inside the freezer typically means the defrost heater, defrost thermostat, or defrost timer has failed. The automatic defrost cycle stops running, and ice accumulates on the evaporator coils. We identify which defrost component failed and replace it to restore normal operation.

Water Leaking on Floor

Water pooling under or in front of the refrigerator usually comes from a clogged defrost drain, a cracked water inlet valve, or a damaged water line. We trace the source, clear the blockage or replace the failed part, and test for leaks before we leave.

Strange Noises

Buzzing, clicking, humming, or rattling can point to a dying compressor, a worn condenser fan motor, or an evaporator fan hitting ice. We isolate the noise source and replace the offending component.

Refrigerator Runs Constantly

If the compressor never cycles off, the unit is working overtime to maintain temperature. This spikes your electric bill and shortens the compressor's life. Common causes include dirty condenser coils, a failing door gasket, or a bad thermostat.

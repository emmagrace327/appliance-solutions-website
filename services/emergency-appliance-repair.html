Quick Answer: What Counts as an Appliance Emergency?

An appliance emergency is any breakdown that risks food spoilage, water damage, gas leaks, or safety hazards if not fixed immediately. A leaking dishwasher flooding your kitchen, a refrigerator full of food that stopped cooling, or a gas range that smells like gas all need same-day repair. Common Emergency 24/7 Appliance Repair Problems We Fix

Refrigerator Emergency

A refrigerator failure means hundreds of dollars in groceries at risk. We prioritize fridge calls because every hour counts. Our technicians carry compressor relays, thermostats, and fan motors to resolve most cooling emergencies on the first visit.

Washer Flooding

A washing machine that won't stop filling or leaks large amounts of water can cause significant floor and subfloor damage. Shut off the water supply valves behind the washer and call us immediately. We'll stop the leak and repair the failed component.

Gas Appliance Emergency

If you smell gas from your stove, oven, or dryer, turn off the gas supply valve, ventilate the area, and call us. Gas leaks from a failed valve, loose connection, or cracked gas line require immediate professional attention.

Dryer Overheating / Burning Smell

A dryer that produces a burning odor or gets dangerously hot to the touch may have a lint fire developing in the vent system, a failed cycling thermostat, or a seized drum bearing. Stop using the dryer and call us right away.

Dishwasher Leak Emergency

A dishwasher that leaks onto your kitchen floor can damage cabinets, flooring, and the subfloor underneath. Turn off the dishwasher and the water supply line under the sink, then call for repair. We'll identify whether the pump, door gasket, or hose connection failed.

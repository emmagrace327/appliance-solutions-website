Common Dryer Repair Problems We Fix

Dryer Not Heating

The most common dryer complaint. In electric dryers, the heating element, thermal fuse, or thermostat usually fails. In gas dryers, the igniter, gas valve coils, or flame sensor are the typical culprits. We test all components and replace only what's needed.

Dryer Takes Too Long

Clothes still damp after a full cycle? A clogged lint trap, restricted vent line, worn drum seals that leak hot air, or a failing heating element are common causes. We also check your dryer vent run — lint buildup in the vent line dramatically reduces drying performance.

Dryer Won't Tumble

If the motor runs but the drum doesn't spin, a broken drum belt is the usual cause. Other possibilities include a worn drum roller, a seized idler pulley, or a failed motor. Belt replacements are among our quickest repairs.

Unusual Noises

Squealing, thumping, or grinding typically points to worn drum support rollers, a stretched or fraying belt, or a worn-out idler pulley. These are mechanical parts that wear over time and are straightforward to replace.

Dryer Shuts Off Mid-Cycle

A dryer that runs for a few minutes then stops is usually overheating due to restricted airflow. A clogged lint screen, blocked vent line, or bad cycling thermostat causes the high-limit safety switch to trip. We clear the airflow issue and test the safety components.

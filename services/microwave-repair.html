Common Microwave Repair Problems We Fix

Microwave Not Heating

The magnetron is the component that produces microwave energy. When it fails, the unit runs but food stays cold. Other causes include a faulty diode, a burned-out capacitor, or a failed transformer. These are high-voltage components that require professional replacement.

Sparking Inside Microwave

Sparks can come from a damaged waveguide cover, chipped interior paint exposing metal, a failing stirrer motor, or arcing from the magnetron. Continued use with sparking can damage the magnetron permanently - call for repair promptly.

Turntable Not Rotating

A stuck turntable is usually caused by a broken coupler, a failed turntable motor, or debris under the turntable tray. If the motor runs but the plate doesn't turn, the drive coupler has likely cracked.

Display or Control Panel Issues

A dead or flickering display, buttons that don't respond, or phantom operation points to a failed control board or membrane keypad. We can replace the control panel assembly to restore full function.

Door Won't Close or Latch

Microwave doors have multiple safety interlock switches. If the door doesn't latch properly, the microwave won't run - this is a built-in safety feature. We replace door latches, hinges, and interlock switches.

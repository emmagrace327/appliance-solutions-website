Maytag Appliance Error Codes — Complete Repair Reference

Field-tested error code reference from our Northern Virginia repair technicians.

Maytag appliances (manufactured by Whirlpool Corporation) use a similar F/E error code system as Whirlpool but with some Maytag-specific variations. This guide covers the codes our technicians encounter regularly on Maytag washers, dryers, dishwashers, and ranges across Northern Virginia homes.

Maytag Washer Error Codes

Maytag Bravos, Centennial, and commercial-grade washers display F/E codes during or after cycles.

Code Meaning What It Means & Repair Cost
F0 E2 Excess Suds Too much detergent used. Switch to HE detergent and reduce amount. The washer adds extra rinse cycles to clear suds.
F3 E1 Pressure Switch Error The water level sensor has failed. The pressure hose may be kinked or clogged. Sensor: $60–$100.
F5 E2 Door Lock Error The front-load door lock is not engaging. Items stuck in the door gasket can cause this. Lock assembly: $80–$150.
F5 E3 Lid Lock Error (Top Load) The lid lock mechanism has failed. Common on Bravos models. Lid lock: $60–$120.
F7 E1 Motor Fault The drive motor is not reaching proper speed. Check the rotor position sensor and motor control board. Repair: $100–$280.
F8 E1 No Water Detected Water is not entering the washer. Check supply valves and clean inlet screens. Inlet valve: $90–$160.
F9 E1 Long Drain The drain cycle exceeded 8 minutes. Clean the drain pump filter and check the drain hose. Pump: $120–$200.
Sd Suds Detected Similar to F0 E2 but a softer warning. Reduce detergent and switch to HE formula.
F7 E5 Shifter Fault The transmission shifter/actuator has failed. The washer cannot switch between agitate and spin. Actuator: $100–$180.

Maytag Dryer Error Codes

Maytag dryers share many codes with Whirlpool since they are built on the same platform.

Code Meaning What It Means & Repair Cost
F2 E4 Exhaust Restriction Dryer vent blockage detected. Professional vent cleaning required to restore airflow and prevent fire.
F3 E1 Exhaust Thermistor Temperature sensor open circuit. Sensor: $50–$90.
F3 E3 Inlet Thermistor The inlet temperature sensor has failed. Sensor: $50–$90.
F4 E1 No Heat Electric: heating element open. Gas: igniter or solenoid failure. Element: $100–$180.
F4 E3 Inlet Temperature High The dryer is overheating at the inlet. Often caused by restricted venting. Clean the vent system.
F6 E2 UI/MCU Communication The user interface board and motor control board have lost communication.

Maytag Dishwasher Error Codes

Maytag dishwashers display error codes or use LED blink patterns depending on the model.

Code Meaning What It Means & Repair Cost
F1 E1 Temperature Sensor NTC sensor failure. The dishwasher cannot monitor water temperature. Sensor: $50–$90.
F5 E2 Door Latch Error The door latch switch is not engaging. Check for obstructions. Latch: $50–$100.
F6 E4 No Water Fill Water not entering the unit. Check supply valve and clean inlet filter screen.
F8 E1 Slow Drain Water draining too slowly. Clean the drain path, check pump and hose.
F8 E4 Overflow Warning Float switch triggered — too much water in the tub. Float or inlet valve issue.
F9 E1 Diverter Disc Error The diverter that routes water to upper/lower spray arms has failed. Motor: $80–$140.

Maytag Oven and Range Error Codes

Maytag ovens use the same F-code system as Whirlpool ranges.

Code Meaning What It Means & Repair Cost
F1 E0 EEPROM Fault Control board memory corrupted. Board replacement: $150–$300.
F2 E0 Oven Over Temperature Oven exceeded safe temperature. Do not use until inspected. Thermostat or relay failure.
F3 E0 Open RTD Sensor Oven temperature probe has an open circuit. Probe: $40–$80.
F3 E1 Shorted RTD Probe has a short circuit. Same replacement.
F5 E1 Door Lock Failure Self-clean door lock malfunction. Lock motor: $80–$160.
F2 E1 Keypad Shorted A button on the oven keypad is stuck or the membrane overlay is damaged. Overlay/panel: $80–$160.

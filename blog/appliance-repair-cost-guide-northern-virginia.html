Repair vs. Replace: The 50% Rule

A good rule of thumb: if the repair cost exceeds 50% of the price of a comparable new appliance, and the unit has passed 75% of its expected lifespan, replacement is usually the smarter investment. We always give honest advice on whether repair or replacement makes more sense for your specific situation.

Kitchen Appliance Safety: Gas Leaks, Electrical Hazards, and Fire Prevention

By Appliance Solutions Inc | May 2026 | Woodbridge, VA

Kitchen Appliance Safety Essentials - Quick Reference

Kitchen appliances cause over 150,000 house fires per year in the United States. Cooking is the #1 cause of home fires, and faulty appliances contribute to thousands of gas leaks and electrical incidents. Knowing the warning signs and what to do in an emergency can save your home and your family. If you smell gas or see sparking, call our 24/7 emergency repair line immediately.

Gas Appliance Safety

Gas stoves, ovens, and dryers should be inspected annually. The gas connector (flex line) behind your stove can crack over time, especially if the stove has been moved. Warning signs of a gas leak: rotten egg smell, hissing sound near connections, dead plants near the appliance, or a higher-than-normal gas bill.

If you smell gas: Do NOT flip any light switches, use your phone, or create any spark. Open windows, leave the house, and call 911 from outside. After the utility company confirms it's safe, call us for emergency gas appliance repair.

Electrical Safety

Major appliances like refrigerators, washers, and dishwashers should be on dedicated circuits. Warning signs of electrical problems: tripped breakers, warm or discolored outlets, burning smell from an appliance, or flickering lights when an appliance starts. Never use extension cords with major appliances.

Older homes in Springfield and Alexandria may have outdated 2-prong outlets or insufficient amperage for modern appliances. If your breaker trips repeatedly, have an electrician upgrade the circuit before the wiring overheats.

Dryer Fire Prevention

Dryers cause approximately 2,900 house fires per year, almost all from lint buildup in the vent line. Clean your lint trap after every load, but more importantly, have the entire dryer vent line professionally cleaned at least once a year. Read our complete dryer vent fire prevention guide for detailed steps.

Water Damage Prevention

Dishwashers and washing machines can leak slowly for weeks before you notice. Inspect under and behind these appliances monthly. Replace rubber washing machine hoses with braided stainless steel hoses (they last longer and burst-proof). For dishwashers, check the door gasket for cracks and the drain hose for kinks.

When to Call an Emergency Technician

Call our 24/7 emergency line if you experience: gas smell from any appliance, sparking or arcing from an appliance, a burning electrical smell, water flooding from a washer or dishwasher, or a refrigerator leaking refrigerant (chemical smell). We respond to emergencies across Woodbridge, Fairfax, Manassas, and all of Northern Virginia within 60 minutes.

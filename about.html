About Appliance Solutions Inc

A locally owned appliance repair company built on honest work, fair pricing, and the kind of service that earns repeat customers.

Last Updated: May 2026

Who We Are

Appliance Solutions Inc is a full-service appliance repair company headquartered in Woodbridge, Virginia. We specialize in diagnosing and repairing residential appliances across Northern Virginia, covering every community within the 571 and 703 area codes.

Our team of licensed, insured technicians handles everything from refrigerators that won't cool to dryers that won't heat, dishwashers that won't drain, and ovens that won't reach temperature. We work on all major brands, carry common replacement parts on every service truck, and complete most repairs in a single visit.

Why We Started This Company

If you've ever waited around all day for a repair person who showed up late, quoted a price that didn't match the phone estimate, and then said they'd need to "order a part and come back next week" — you understand exactly why Appliance Solutions Inc exists.

We launched this company because Northern Virginia deserved an appliance repair service that actually respects people's time and money. That means showing up within the scheduled window, giving you an honest diagnosis you can understand, quoting a price before we touch a single tool, and finishing the job right the first time.

What Makes Us Different

There are plenty of appliance repair companies in the Woodbridge area. Here's what separates us from the rest:

  • Genuine 24/7 availability. We don't just put "24/7" on our website and go home at 5pm. Our dispatch takes calls around the clock. If your freezer dies at midnight, we answer.
  • Stocked service trucks. Our vehicles carry over 200 of the most commonly needed parts for Samsung, LG, Whirlpool, GE, Maytag, and other top brands. This means fewer return trips and faster fixes.
  • Transparent pricing. Our $89 diagnostic fee applies toward the repair cost if you approve the work. No surprise charges, no "additional labor" tacked on at the end.
  • Background-checked technicians. Every person who enters your home has been vetted, licensed, and insured. That's not a marketing line — it's a requirement for everyone on our team.
  • Local ownership. We're based right here at 3030 Ps Business Center Dr in Woodbridge. When you call us, you're talking to our team, not a national answering service that subcontracts to whoever happens to be in the area.

Appliances We Repair

Our technicians are trained on virtually every type of household appliance:

  • Refrigerators and freezers (all configurations)
